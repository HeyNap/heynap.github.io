Questions

How does HeyNap plan naps?

It starts from what is typical for your baby's age: how long they can stay awake, how many naps they take and how long. As you log naps and bedtimes, the plan follows your baby's own rhythm, and it updates through the day from what you log.

What ages is HeyNap for?

From birth to two years. Under two months, babies don't keep a schedule yet, so the plan is only a guide: follow your baby's sleepy cues.

Is HeyNap free?

Logging sleep, feeds, diapers, health and growth is free. The plan, nap predictions, reminders, your stats and most sounds are part of a subscription.

Is HeyNap medical advice?

No. HeyNap gives general guidance on when your baby may be ready to sleep. It doesn't diagnose anything. If you're worried about your baby's sleep or health, talk to your pediatrician.
