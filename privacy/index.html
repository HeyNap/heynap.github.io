Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

HeyNap is an app for parents and caregivers. It tracks a baby's sleep, feeds, diapers, health and growth, and plans naps and bedtime. It is published by DIACONU MOBILE APPS SOCIETATE CU RASPUNDERE LIMITATA ("Diaconu Mobile Apps", "we", "us"). On Google Play, our developer name is PostMuse.

This policy covers the HeyNap app and the website heynap.app. It explains what information is collected, where it is stored, who processes it for us, and the choices and rights you have. We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

  • Everything you log about your baby is stored only on your phone. It isn't sent to us or anyone else, and we can't see it.
  • HeyNap has no accounts.
  • The app sends usage data and, for some sessions, screen recordings to Google Firebase and Amplitude. Usage data never includes your baby's name, your notes, or health details, and recordings hide all text on screen.
  • When the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Sentry and Firebase Crashlytics, without names, notes or anything you logged.
  • You can turn usage data off in the app's settings.
  • If you allow notifications, we can send you messages through Firebase.
  • Purchases go through the App Store and RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

1. What we collect

1.1 What you enter in the app: stays on your phone

To plan your baby's day and keep a record of it, the app asks for and stores:

  • About you and your baby: your first name, your baby's name, birthday or due date, and sex if you choose to give it.
  • Sleep: wake-ups, naps, bedtimes and night wakings, with their times and where your baby slept.
  • Feeds and diapers: nursing, bottles (amount and type of milk), solids, pumping, and diapers.
  • Routine: baths, time outside, vitamins and supplements.
  • Health: temperatures, medicines and doses, symptoms, vaccines and doctor visits.
  • Growth: weight, height, head size and teeth.
  • Notes you add to any entry, and your settings and plan preferences (units, clock, nap and bedtime choices).

All of this is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. The plan, the stats and the reminders are all worked out on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to any of our service providers. Some of it is health information about your child, and it stays under your control.

Your phone's own backups (iCloud or Google) may include the app's data, depending on your phone's settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their terms.

1.2 Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are made through the App Store (or Google Play). Apple or Google handle the payment; we never receive your card details. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, receives a random app user identifier, the products you buy, transaction identifiers, and your subscription's status, so the app knows what you have unlocked.

1.3 Usage data and session recordings

To understand how HeyNap is used, the app sends usage data to Google Firebase Analytics and Amplitude:

  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language, region and time zone.
  • Approximate location: country and region, worked out from your IP address. We never collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Usage events: how the app is used: which screens are opened and which buttons are pressed; your onboarding choices (your goals, your baby's usual wake-up time, number and length of naps, bedtime, and whether you gave names); your settings and the plan values you set; which sounds you play; notifications received and opened; and purchases started, completed or restored.
  • About your logs: the kind of entry (a nap, a feed, a diaper), its time of day and length, and whether it has a note. For sleep, feeds, diapers and the routine, also its details: amounts (rounded), the type of milk, the type of diaper, where a nap happened. And how the plan's predictions compared with what you logged: how many minutes early or late.
  • About your baby: age in weeks and months (never the birthday), whether you're expecting and how many weeks are left, and your baby's sex if you set it.
  • Session recordings: for a share of sessions, Amplitude records what is on screen as you use the app, so we can see where the app is hard to use. All text on screen is hidden in these recordings, including names and notes. Drawings, such as the day ring and the charts in Stats, are not hidden, so a recording can show your baby's day on the ring, with the times drawn around it, or a chart with its figures.
  • Identifiers: random identifiers that these services create for your app installation.

Usage events never include your baby's name, your notes, the birthday, or the details of health and growth entries (temperatures, medicines, symptoms, vaccines, doctor visits, weight, height, head size, teeth): for those, only that an entry was logged. We use this data to understand how the app is used, not to profile you. You can turn it off at any time with Share usage data in the app's settings. We don't use the advertising identifier and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1.4 Crash reports and performance

When the app crashes, runs into an error or slows down, it sends a report to Sentry and Google Firebase Crashlytics, so we can fix it:

  • What went wrong: the error, where in the app's code it happened, and the steps just before it: the app's own messages, the screens opened, which kind of button was tapped (never the words on it), and the app going to the background or running low on memory.
  • The screen: a screenshot and a short recording of the moments before the error, and the layout of the screen (which parts of the app were shown, without their text). All text and images are hidden in them; drawings, such as the day ring and the charts in Stats, are not (see 1.3).
  • Performance: how long the app takes to start and screens to load, frozen or slow frames, the app hanging, and where in its code the app spends its time.
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, and free memory and storage.
  • Identifiers: random identifiers that these services create for your app installation.

With Share usage data on, Sentry also records whole sessions (all of them, or a share we set), in the same way as the recordings in 1.3.

These reports never include your baby's name, your notes, or anything else you wrote or logged, except what a drawing shows on screen. Like any request over the internet, sending one shows your IP address to the provider; we don't use it to identify you. They are sent for every user, because we need them to keep the app working; if you object, write to us (section 13).

1.5 Remote settings

When it starts, the app downloads our own configuration from Google Firebase Remote Config. It holds settings of ours, not data about you: for example how many sessions are recorded, or which version of the plan a group of users gets, so we can compare them. Some settings differ between groups of users, so to pick the right ones the request carries your app installation's random identifier and the app's version, language and country. It isn't usage data, so Share usage data doesn't affect it.

1.6 Reminders and notifications

Reminders about naps and bedtime are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. Nothing about them is sent to us.

The app also registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your phone a push token. We use it to send you messages from us, such as news about HeyNap or tips, either to everyone or to a group chosen from usage data (for example, parents of babies of a certain age). They show only if you allow notifications. Receiving or opening a message is recorded in usage data, never its text. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

1.7 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, and we use it only to answer you.

1.8 The website

heynap.app is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

1.9 What we don't collect

HeyNap has no accounts, so it never asks for your email address or a password. It doesn't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location, and it doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.

4. Who else sees your data

  • When the law requires it: we disclose data if a valid legal request obliges us to, and only what it requires.
  • If the company changes hands: if HeyNap is sold or merged, the data we hold moves with it, under this policy, and we'll tell you before that happens.

We don't sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

5. How long it's kept

  • What you log, on your phone: until you delete the entry, erase all data, or delete the app.
  • Usage data: up to 24 months. Session recordings: up to 90 days.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Push token: until you turn notifications off or delete the app, after which it stops working and Firebase removes it.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Emails: for as long as needed to help you, then up to 2 years.

6. Deleting your data

  • Delete any entry in the app, from its editor.
  • Delete everything with Erase all data in the app's settings: your logs, your answers and your settings are deleted from your phone.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that phone. Copies in your phone's backups follow your backup settings.
  • To delete the usage data, session recordings and crash reports linked to your app installation, write to us (section 13); turning off Share usage data stops new usage data and recordings.

7. Security

What you log is protected by your phone's own security (its passcode and storage encryption). Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit. Your baby's information is never used for advertising or sold.

8. Children

HeyNap is meant for adults: parents and other caregivers. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. The information about your baby is entered by you, as their parent or caregiver, and stays on your phone. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we'll delete it.

9. Your rights

9.1 EU, EEA and UK

You have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You can also complain to a data protection authority: ours is the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), or you can contact the authority where you live.

9.2 California and other US states

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to access, correct and delete it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

9.3 How to use them

What you log is on your phone, where you can see, change and delete it yourself. To get a copy, tap Download my data in the app's settings: you get everything you logged, your answers and your settings as a JSON file and a spreadsheet (CSV), to keep or to move to another app. For what we hold (purchase records, usage data, session recordings and crash reports), write to us at the address in section 13. We answer within 30 days, and may ask for details that help us find your data.

10. International transfers

We are based in Romania. Amplitude and Sentry store HeyNap's data in the EU. Firebase and RevenueCat process data in the United States and other countries. For those transfers, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Not medical advice

HeyNap gives general guidance on sleep timing. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything. See our Terms of Use.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when HeyNap changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we add a feature that changes how your data is handled, such as accounts or sync, we'll update this policy and tell you in the app before it takes effect.
