Your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple or Google:

  • iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, or go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

New phone? Open the subscription screen in HeyNap and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought with.

Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google (Google Play refunds).

Get a copy of your data

Open Settings and tap Download my data. You get two files: everything you logged, your answers and your settings (JSON), and your logs as a spreadsheet (CSV). Save them to Files, mail them, or send them by AirDrop.

Delete your data

HeyNap has no accounts: everything you log is stored only on your phone.

  • In the app: open Settings and tap Erase all data. Your logs, your answers and your settings are deleted from your phone.
  • Deleting the app also deletes everything it stored on that phone. Copies in your phone's backups follow your backup settings.
  • Usage data, session recordings and crash reports: turn off Share usage data in the app's settings to stop new usage data and session recordings, then write to postmuseapp+privacy@gmail.com with the subject "HeyNap: delete my data", and we'll delete what is linked to your app installation.

Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Erasing your data or deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription: cancel it as described above. Our Privacy Policy has the details.

About health

HeyNap gives general guidance on sleep timing, not medical advice. If you're worried about your baby's sleep or health, talk to your pediatrician. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
